Ask us directly ↗
Are quotes really free? +

Yes. Aegis Safety provides free, no-obligation quotes. We’ll discuss your needs and proposed scope before providing job-specific pricing.

How do you determine pricing? +

Every job is priced individually based on the agreed scope, document volume, complexity, industry, and deliverables. You’ll receive a tailored quote before work begins.

Can I request a construction audit? +

Yes. Choose “Construction audit” in the enquiry form and describe the type of audit, site, or documentation you have in mind. We’ll discuss scope and suitability before preparing a free quote.

Does a review or audit guarantee compliance? +

No. A review or audit cannot guarantee compliance or eliminate workplace risk. The scope and applicable requirements should be agreed in advance, and the service does not replace an organization’s legal responsibilities.

What should I send before we begin? +

Usually, a brief description of your operations, the service you’re interested in, locations involved, and your main concerns. Avoid sending confidential information until a secure process is agreed.

How do we get started? +

Send an enquiry using the form below. We’ll follow up to discuss your project and prepare a free, no-obligation quote tailored to the job.
